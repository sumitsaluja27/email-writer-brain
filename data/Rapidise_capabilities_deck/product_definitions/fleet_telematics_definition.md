# Fleet Telematics Product Definition

## What is Fleet Telematics?
Fleet telematics combines GPS tracking, vehicle diagnostics, driver behavior monitoring, and communication to manage commercial vehicle fleets effectively.

## Who Buys Fleet Telematics?

### Primary Customers (ODM Opportunities)
1. **Fleet Management Service Providers** - Offer telematics as a service
2. **Trucking Companies** - Manage long-haul fleets
3. **Logistics Companies** - Track deliveries and optimize routes
4. **Rental Car Companies** - Monitor rental vehicles
5. **Insurance Companies** - Usage-based insurance programs

### Product Names & Keywords
- Fleet telematics

### Related Products (Often Bundled)
- Dashcams (for video evidence)
- GPS trackers (for location)
- Driver monitoring (for safety)

### NOT a Fleet Telematics Company
- Consumer GPS navigation
- Personal vehicle tracking
- Public transit management
