# IP Camera / Surveillance Camera Product Definition

## What is an IP Camera?
An IP camera (network camera) is a digital video camera that transmits data over a network. Used for security surveillance in homes, businesses, and public spaces.

## Who Buys IP Cameras?

### Primary Customers (ODM Opportunities)
1. **Security System Integrators** - Install complete surveillance systems
2. **Consumer Electronics Brands** - Sell home security cameras
3. **Commercial Security Companies** - Enterprise surveillance
4. **Smart Home Companies** - Integrate with home automation
5. **Retail Chains** - Loss prevention systems

### Product Names & Keywords
- IP camera, Network camera
- CCTV camera, CCTV system
- Surveillance camera
- Security camera
- Video surveillance
- NVR (Network Video Recorder)
- PTZ camera (Pan-Tilt-Zoom)
- Video monitoring

### Related Industries
- Security & investigations
- Consumer electronics
- Electrical/electronic manufacturing
- Smart home
- Retail

### NOT an IP Camera Company
- Body cameras (wearable)
- Dashcams (vehicle-mounted)
- Webcams (for video conferencing)
- Broadcast cameras
- Photography cameras
