# Body Camera Product Definition

## What is a Body Camera?
A body camera (body-worn camera, BWC) is a wearable recording device typically mounted on clothing, helmet, or glasses. Used primarily by law enforcement, security personnel, and first responders.

## Who Buys Body Cameras?

### Primary Customers (ODM Opportunities)
1. **Law Enforcement Equipment Suppliers** - Sell to police departments
2. **Security Companies** - Equip private security guards
3. **Government Contractors** - Supply to military, border patrol
4. **Healthcare Equipment Providers** - Paramedics, hospital security

### Product Names & Keywords
- Body camera, Body cam, Bodycam
- Body-worn camera, BWC
- Police camera
- Wearable camera
- Law enforcement camera
- Security camera (wearable)

### Related Industries
- Security & investigations
- Law enforcement
- Defense & space
- Government administration
- Healthcare (security)

### NOT a Body Camera Company
- Action cameras for sports (GoPro-style)
- Dashboard cameras
- Fixed security cameras
- Webcams
