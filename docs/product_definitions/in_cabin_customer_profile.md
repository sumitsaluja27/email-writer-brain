# In-Cabin / DMS Customer Profile

## Customer Type: ODM Customer (Special Case)
In-cabin/DMS companies may need DIFFERENT things from Rapidise:
- Some need complete finished cameras
- Some need only software licensing
- Some need PCB design + manufacturing (they do integration)

---

## REAL Website Examples

### Example 1: Smart Eye (smarteye.se)

**What they do:**
> Driver monitoring (DMS) - Eye tracking technology for automotive

**Their business model:**
- Provide SOFTWARE + ALGORITHMS for driver monitoring
- May license technology to OEMs
- May need hardware partner for complete solution

**Why they're a customer:**
- They have the AI/software, may need camera hardware
- Could be partner for Rapidise's DMS cameras

---

### Example 2: Seeing Machines

**What they do:**
> Driver monitoring technology for automotive and fleet

**Products:**
- Guardian (fleet DMS)
- Automotive DMS for OEMs

---

## Pattern Summary: What Makes an In-Cabin/DMS Customer Website

| Element | What to Look For |
|---------|------------------|
| **Headline** | "Driver monitoring", "DMS", "fatigue detection", "distraction" |
| **Technology** | Eye tracking, AI, computer vision, in-cabin sensing |
| **Customers** | Automotive OEMs, fleet companies |
| **Products** | May show software platform, not just hardware |

---

## Important: Different Customer Needs

| Type | What They Need from Rapidise |
|------|------------------------------|
| **Software-only company** | Complete camera hardware with their software |
| **OEM supplier** | PCB design + manufacturing, they do integration |
| **Fleet solution provider** | Finished DMS camera product |

---

## Questions to Ask These Customers:

1. Do you need a complete finished camera?
2. Do you need PCB design + manufacturing?
3. Do you license software to hardware partners?

---

## Who Is NOT an In-Cabin Customer

- Generic automotive suppliers (make many parts)
- Chip companies (make processors, not cameras)
- Pure research companies (not manufacturing products)
