# Dashcam Product Definition

## What is a Dashcam?
A dashcam (dashboard camera) is a vehicle-mounted video camera that continuously records the view through the front windshield. Some models also include rear-facing or cabin-facing cameras.

## Who Buys Dashcams?

### Primary Customers (ODM Opportunities)
1. **Fleet Management Companies** - Manage trucks, delivery vehicles, taxis, ride-share fleets
2. **Consumer Electronics Brands** - Sell directly to consumers
3. **Automotive Accessories Companies** - Sell through retail channels
4. **Insurance Companies** - Provide to policyholders for discounts
5. **Telematics Providers** - Bundle with GPS tracking solutions

### Product Names & Keywords
- Dashcam, Dash cam, Dash camera
- Car DVR, Car recorder
- Driving recorder
- Fleet camera
- Vehicle camera
- Automotive camera

### Related Industries
- Automotive
- Fleet management
- Consumer electronics
- Telematics
- Insurance technology

### NOT a Dashcam Company
- Software-only analytics platforms
- Cloud storage providers
- GPS tracking (without camera)
- General security cameras (not vehicle-mounted)
