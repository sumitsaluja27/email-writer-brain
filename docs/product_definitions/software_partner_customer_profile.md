# Software Partner Customer Profile

## Customer Type: Software/Platform Partner
Companies that have **software, AI, or platform** but may need:
- **Hardware manufacturing partner** (ODM)
- **Software development support** (new features, updates)
- **Full solution partnership** (hardware + software)

---

## Why These Companies Matter

| Their Strength | Their Gap | Rapidise Value |
|---------------|-----------|----------------|
| AI algorithms | No camera manufacturing | ODM hardware |
| Fleet platform | No in-house devices | White-label cameras |
| DMS software | Need hardware integration | Camera modules |
| Cloud platform | Need edge devices | IoT devices + software |

---

## REAL Company Examples

### Example 1: Smart Eye (smarteye.se)

**What they have:**
- Driver monitoring (DMS) software
- Eye tracking AI algorithms
- Automotive OEM relationships

**What they may need:**
- Camera hardware for complete DMS solution
- In-cabin camera manufacturing
- Software integration support

**Rapidise fit:** Provide DMS camera hardware with their software

## Pattern Summary: What Makes a Software Partner Website

| Element | What to Look For |
|---------|------------------|
| **Headline** | "Platform", "Software", "AI-powered", "Solution" |
| **Products** | Software/SaaS, algorithms, cloud platform |
| **Key terms** | "We integrate with...", "Partner ecosystem", "API" |
| **Missing** | No hardware products page, no "buy device" |
| **Industries** | Fleet, automotive, security, logistics |

---

## Different Partnership Needs

| Company Type | What They Need | Rapidise Offer |
|-------------|----------------|----------------|
| **DMS Software Co** | Camera hardware | In-cabin camera ODM |
| **Fleet Platform** | Dashcams to bundle | White-label dashcams |
| **Security Platform** | IP cameras | Camera ODM + cloud SW |
| **IoT Platform** | Edge devices | Beacons, trackers |

---

## Key Questions to Ask These Leads

1. "Do you currently manufacture your own hardware?"
2. "Are you looking for a hardware partner?"
3. "Do you need software development support?"
4. "What's your time-to-market requirement?"

---

## Who Is NOT a Software Partner

- Pure SaaS with no hardware need (CRM, marketing tools)
- Software that doesn't relate to cameras/tracking
- Companies already manufacturing in-house
