# Body Camera Customer Profile

## Customer Types: ODM Customer + End Users
- **ODM Customers** = Companies that SELL body cameras (Axon, HALO, Reveal Media)
- **End Users** = Organizations that BUY body cameras (Police departments, G4S security)

---

## REAL Website Examples - ODM Customers

### Example 1: Axon (axon.com)

**Main headline:**
> "Protect more lives in more places"

**Sub-headline:**
> "Experience the leading operating system for public safety"

**Product categories:**
- TASER ENERGY DEVICES
- **CAMERAS & SENSORS**
- TRAINING
- DIGITAL EVIDENCE MANAGEMENT
- DRONES & ROBOTICS
- Records Management
- REAL-TIME OPERATIONS

**Camera products:**
- **Axon Body 4** (body camera)
- Axon Fleet 3 (in-car video)
- Axon Outpost
- Axon Lightpost

**Key roles in the company:**
- Director of Product, Body Worn Cameras
- VP Devices Product Management
- Sr. Director Engineering, Connected Devices
- VP Hardware Engineering

**Why they're a customer:**
- $2B+ revenue, world leader in body cams
- Full product line: cameras, Tasers, evidence management
- May need ODM partner for specific lines

## REAL Website Examples - End Users

### Example 1: G4S (g4s.com)

**Main services:**
- **Security Services** - "extensive range of security systems and products"
- Cash Solutions - cash management
- Consulting Services - risk consulting
- Care and Justice services

**Company profile:**
- $20B revenue, 800,000 employees globally
- World's largest security company
- Security guards USE body cameras
- Operates in law enforcement, prisons, security

**Why they're an end user:**
- They BUY body cameras for their security guards
- May also specify/resell cameras as part of contracts
- Huge volume buyer

---

### Example 2: Edmonton Police Service (edmontonpolice.ca)

**What they are:**
> Canadian police department

**Company profile:**
- $397M budget, 3,000 employees
- Law enforcement agency

**Why they're an end user:**
- Police departments BUY body cameras from Axon, HALO, etc.
- They don't manufacture or sell body cams
- Pitch: Sell Rapidise body cams TO them

**Company profile:**

## Pattern Summary: What Makes a Body Cam ODM Customer Website

| Element | What to Look For |
|---------|------------------|
| **Headline** | "Protect", "public safety", "body camera" |
| **Products page** | Camera models with specs (Axon Body 4, Fleet) |
| **Product categories** | CAMERAS, DEVICES, EVIDENCE MANAGEMENT |
| **Industries** | Law enforcement, security, healthcare |
| **Key roles** | VP Product, VP Hardware, Director Engineering |

---

## Pattern Summary: What Makes a Body Cam End User Website

| Element | What to Look For |
|---------|------------------|
| **Headline** | "Police department", "security services", "guarding" |
| **Services** | Security guards, cash management, justice services |
| **NO products page** | They don't sell cameras |
| **Industries** | Law enforcement, security guarding, corrections |

---

## Who Is NOT a Body Cam Customer

- Consumer action camera brands (GoPro - different market)
- Dashcam companies (different product category)
- General electronics without body cam focus
- Software-only companies (video analytics)
