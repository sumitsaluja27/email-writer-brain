# Beacon Customer Profile

## Customer Type: END USER (Not ODM)
These companies BUY beacons/trackers to use in their operations. They are potential BUYERS of Rapidise beacons, not ODM manufacturing customers.

---

## Who Is a Beacon End User?

### Cold Storage / Warehousing
Companies that need to track temperature-sensitive goods.

**Examples from our sample:**
- USA Cold Storage - Cold storage warehouse. Uses beacons for tracking.
- VersaCold Logistics Services - Cold chain logistics
- Vertical Cold Storage - Cold storage warehouse
- Interstate Warehousing - Warehousing
- KRYOTEC - Cold chain pharma

**Why they need beacons:**
- Track temperature of refrigerated goods
- Asset tracking in warehouses
- Forklift/equipment tracking
- Compliance monitoring

---

## What Their Website Looks Like

### Homepage has:
- "Cold storage", "warehousing", "logistics" in headline
- Photos of warehouses, trucks, forklifts
- Services: storage, transport, fulfillment

### NOT on their website:
- Products page with beacon hardware (they don't SELL beacons)
- Pricing for tracker devices
- Hardware specs

---

## Important Distinction

**For email outreach to beacon leads:**
- These are potential CUSTOMERS buying from Rapidise
- Pitch: "We make beacons that help you track assets"
- NOT: "We can manufacture beacons for your brand"

**For finding MORE beacon leads:**
- Look for: Logistics, warehousing, cold chain companies
- Industries: Food distribution, pharma, manufacturing
