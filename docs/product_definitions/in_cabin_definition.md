# In-Cabin / Driver Monitoring Product Definition

## What is In-Cabin / DMS?
In-cabin monitoring (Driver Monitoring System / Occupant Monitoring System) uses cameras inside the vehicle to detect driver fatigue, distraction, and ensure safety compliance.

## Who Buys In-Cabin Systems?

### Primary Customers (ODM Opportunities)
1. **Automotive OEMs** - Build into new vehicles
2. **Fleet Management Companies** - Retrofit to existing fleets
3. **ADAS Suppliers** - Part of advanced driver assistance
4. **Commercial Vehicle Manufacturers** - Trucks, buses
5. **Ride-Sharing/Taxi Companies** - Monitor drivers

### Product Names & Keywords
- Driver monitoring, DMS
- Occupant monitoring, OMS
- In-cabin camera
- Cabin camera
- Fatigue detection
- Drowsiness detection
- Driver safety system
- ADAS camera (interior)
- Distraction detection

### Related Industries
- Automotive
- Transportation
- Fleet management
- ADAS/Autonomous driving

### NOT an In-Cabin Company
- External dashcams (front-facing only)
- Rearview cameras
- Parking cameras
- General automotive tier-1 suppliers (unless specifically DMS)
