# Beacon / GPS Tracker Product Definition

## What is a Beacon/Tracker?
A beacon or GPS tracker is a device that transmits location data. Used for asset tracking, fleet management, personal safety, and logistics.

## Who Buys Beacons/Trackers?

### Primary Customers (ODM Opportunities)
1. **Fleet Management Companies** - Track vehicles and deliveries
2. **Logistics & Supply Chain Companies** - Track shipments and containers
3. **Asset Management Companies** - Track equipment, tools, machinery
4. **Personal Safety Device Makers** - Track children, elderly, pets
5. **Construction Companies** - Track heavy equipment

### Product Names & Keywords
- Beacon, BLE beacon
- GPS tracker, GPS tracking device
- Asset tracker
- Fleet tracker
- Vehicle tracker, Vehicle tracking
- Telematics device
- Location tracker
- Tracking device

### Related Industries
- Logistics & supply chain
- Fleet management
- Transportation
- Automotive
- Construction
- Security

### NOT a Beacon/Tracker Company
- Software-only fleet management
- Mapping/navigation apps
- GIS (Geographic Information Systems)
- Banks named "Beacon"
