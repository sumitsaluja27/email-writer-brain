## Pattern Summary: What Makes an IP Camera Customer Website

| Element | What to Look For |
|---------|------------------|
| **Headline** | "Security", "surveillance", "AI-powered", "protect" |
| **Products** | Video security, security cameras, access control |
| **Features** | Cloud storage, AI alerts, mobile app, video search |
| **Industries** | Retail, education, healthcare, enterprise |
| **Call to action** | "Free trial", "Get started", "Shop now" |

---

## Who Is NOT an IP Camera Customer

- End users (banks, schools that USE cameras)
- Installers who only install, don't brand products
- Component suppliers (image sensors, chips)
- Pure software companies (video analytics only)
