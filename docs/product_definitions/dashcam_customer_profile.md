# Dashcam Customer Profile

## Customer Type: ODM Customer
Rapidise manufactures dashcams for these companies. They SELL dashcams under their brand.

## Pattern Summary: What Makes a Dashcam Customer Website

| Element | What to Look For |
|---------|------------------|
| **Headline** | "Fleet", "telematics", "safety", "dash cam" |
| **Products** | AI dash cam, video telematics, fleet cameras |
| **Menu** | Fleet solutions, dash cams, driver safety |
| **Industries** | Trucking, logistics, construction, delivery |
| **Trust signals** | Years in business, # of customers, enterprise logos |

---

## Who Is NOT a Dashcam Customer

- Automotive OEMs (Ford, Toyota) - Too big, design own
- Software-only platforms - No hardware
- Component suppliers - Make sensors, not finished products
- End users - Trucking companies that USE dashcams, don't SELL them
